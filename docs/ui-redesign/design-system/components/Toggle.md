# Toggle

Checkboxes pick items, radios pick one of a few, switches turn a setting on at once.

## Use
- `.mg-check` with a checkbox or radio: 20px box, `btn` fill when checked.
- `.mg-switch` with `role="switch"`: for settings that apply immediately (no Save). The knob springs across (`--mg-spring`, 520ms).
- The label is the whole hit area; write it as the thing that happens ("Email the client").
