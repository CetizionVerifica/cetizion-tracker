# Segmented

The segmented control switches a view's period or mode; tabs switch between sections of one record or list.

## Segmented
- `.mg-seg` on `track`, options 36px high, a `glass-strong` thumb that slides on the soft spring (640ms). Call `MochaGlass.seg(el, i)`.
- Two to four short options. Lives in the control bar or above a chart.

## Tabs
- `.mg-tabs`: text tabs on a `line` rule; the selected one gets a `caramel` underline. Counts use `.mg-count` (caramel when selected).
- Scroll sideways on a phone rather than wrap.
