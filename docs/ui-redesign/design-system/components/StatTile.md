# StatTile

Tiles show the few numbers a screen is about; the strip shows a row of related totals.

## Use
- `.mg-glass.mg-tile`: a `.mg-label`, the figure (`tile-figure`, 32/700, counts up once), and a foot line with a badge or context.
- `.mg-strip`: one glass panel split by `line` rules, figures at 24px. For a record's totals or a list's summary.
- Expected money is `caramel-text` in numbers and hatched (`.mg-hatch`) in bars, everywhere.
- `.mg-progress`: `figure` for money in, hatch for expected, on a `track`.
- Use tiles only when the numbers are the point of the screen; three or four, never a wall.
