# Banner

Banners explain something about the page that stays true; toasts confirm what just happened.

## Banners
- `.mg-banner` with a tone (`--late`, `--wait`, `--ok`, default info): an icon, a bold first line, one sentence on what to do, and the action.
- At the top of the page or panel they are about. Never more than one per panel.

## Toasts
- `.mg-toast` in strong glass, bottom centre above the dock, 5s. A dot shows the outcome; Undo when the action can be reversed.
- Words name the result: "Payment recorded", "Reminder sent".
